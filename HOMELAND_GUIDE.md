## 1. Overview & How to Unlock

The **Homeland** is your personal instanced domain in *Aniimo*. Unlike the dangerous wilds of the continent of Idyll, the Homeland is a tranquil, customizable building and farming plot centered around your **RV (Recreational Vehicle)**. 

### How to Unlock:
1. Advance the main story until you reach the **Sea of Flowers**.
2. Complete your **Student Level II** training trials.
3. Talk to the NPC **Fannie** and accept the questline **"Prologue: Wondrous RV"** (or track the quest **"Own Your Homeland"**).
4. Once completed, your Homeland is unlocked permanently and can be accessed from the world map or any fast-travel point.

## 3. Form System & Homeland Impact

Every Aniimo species exists in up to three major forms. Transforming or capturing these forms can dramatically redefine an Aniimo's homeland utility:

```
[Basic Form]  ──────►  [Weather Form]  ──────►  [Prismana Form]
(Standard Habitat)   (Environment Triggered)   (Prismatic Weather / Pact)
```

### 1. Basic Form
* The standard encounter form in temperate weather.
* Provides default elemental affinities and baseline Homeland Ability levels.
* Lumin stage Aniimo have Lv 1 abilities; Gamma stage have Lv 2; Nova stage have Lv 3 to Lv 4.

### 2. Weather Forms (Rainstorm, Thunderstorm, Snowfield, etc.)
* Appear in the wild only under specific weather conditions (Rain, Thunderstorm, Blizzard, Sandstorm, Heatwave, Eclipse).
* **Homeland Shift:** Weather forms often gain a **secondary element** or **upgrade existing ability levels by +1**.
  * *Example:* **Scorchhowl** (Base: Fire Lv 3) becomes **Scorchhowl Thunderstorm** (Fire Lv 3 + Lightning Lv 2), allowing one creature to smelt metals AND power electric generators.
* Once captured, you can morph appearance in the creature menu without losing combat stats.

### 3. Prismana Forms
* The rarest variants, identified by their radiant, shifting rainbow auras.
* Obtained during **Prismana Flow** weather events, through the **Prismana Pact**, or via special in-game events.
* **Homeland Mastery:**
  * **+1 Level Boost** to primary Homeland abilities (can reach maximum Lv 4 and Lv 5).
  * **Exclusive Prismana Traits:** Inherent bonuses like reduced stamina consumption (-25%), immunity to fatigue slumping, or bonus yield chances (+20% to +40%).
  * *Example:* **Prismana Bouldus** reaches **Earth Lv 5**, making it the absolute fastest quarry miner in the entire game.

## 4. The RV (Recreational Vehicle) Progression

The RV is the heart of your base. Upgrading your RV requires **Home Coins** and refined materials (Timber, Stone Bricks, Copper/Iron Ingots, Glass).

### RV Tier Milestones:
* **Tier 1 (Base):** Unlocks 4 Aniimo worker slots, 2 small farm plots, Campfire, and basic workbench.
* **Tier 2:** Unlocks 8 worker slots, Hatchinator slot 1, Water Pump, Masonry Table, and basic Cooler.
* **Tier 3:** Unlocks 12 worker slots, Smeltery, Windmill, secondary Hatchinator slot, and Recreation Tent.
* **Tier 4:** Unlocks 16 worker slots, Electrical Dynamo, Fertilizer Distiller, and Industrial Kitchen.
* **Tier 5 (Mastery):** Unlocks 20 worker slots, 3x Hatchinators, Cryo Vault (zero food spoilage), and Automated Assembly Lines.

## 5. Worker Management, Morale & Fatigue

Working Aniimos have two crucial gauges: **Hunger** and **Sanity/Fatigue**.

### 🍖 Food Management
* Always keep the **Central Food Trough** filled.
* High-tier cooked meals (e.g., *Starlight Stew*, *Spiced Roast*) grant lasting stamina and work-speed buffs.
* Raw berries or uncooked crops will keep workers alive, but their work efficiency drops by 30%.

### 💤 Morale & Sleep
* Workers need beds or designated resting zones.
* Assigning an Aniimo with **Leisure (e.g., Tromber, Stellarys)** to a recreational stage emits an aura that prevents exhaustion spirals.
* Aniimo with the **Dark** element (e.g., Inferlupa, Dreaple) prefer working night shifts and won't suffer dark debuffs.

## 6. Hatchinator (Egg Incubation) Secrets

Eggs gathered from the wild are incubated in the **Hatchinator**:
1. Incubation proceeds in **real time** (even while offline).
2. **Speeding up Incubation:**
   * Place an Aniimo with **Light** (like **Pomegg** or **Luminelle**) nearby to provide warmth.
   * Heated environment boosts incubation for Fire/Earth eggs; cooled environment boosts Ice/Water eggs.
3. **Prismana Hatches:** Hatching eggs has an approximate 2-5% chance to yield a Prismana variant with randomized golden traits.

## 7. Homeland Economy: Home Coins vs Bud Tickets

| Currency | How to Obtain | Primary Use |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Home Coins** | Homeland delivery orders, selling refined raw materials, daily base quests. | Upgrading the RV, unlocking facility blueprints, expanding farm borders. |
| **Bud Tickets** | Homeland events, recreational milestones, flower distillation (**Melloblum** perk). | Buying luxury furniture, cosmetic RV paint jobs, landscaping garden items. |
| **Growth Buds** | Crafted at the Botanical Workbench using excess crops and fertilizer. | Consumed to grant **1,000 EXP** instantly to any Aniimo. |

## 8. Optimal Base Layout & Automation Tips

```
[ Farm Sector ]           [ Industrial Sector ]         [ Logistics & Storage ]
- Grass (Melloblum)        - Fire (Scorchhowl)           - Central Silos & Chests
- Water (Leafy)            - Earth (Bouldus)             - Carry (Tubster)
- Wind (Pomawk)            - Lightning (Turbo)           - Fast Sorting Hub
        │                           │                            ▲
        └───────────────────────────┴────────────────────────────┘
                         Managed & Hauled By Tubster
```

1. **Pair Planting & Irrigation in Proximity:** Position your water source and farm plots side-by-side so Leafy or Pranky doesn't waste time pathfinding.
2. **Assign a Dedicated Hauler:** Always keep at least one high-tier **Carry** specialist (like **Tubster** or **Fenrier**). Otherwise, crafters will stop working when their output bin fills up!
3. **Keep Smelteries Away from Coolers:** Heat stations will increase cooling power drain if placed adjacent to ice boxes.
4. **Use Weather Forms to Consolidate Roles:** Instead of employing one Fire worker and one Lightning worker, use a **Thunderstorm Scorchhowl** to handle both!
