📚 Complete Aniimo Homeland Abilities & Worker Guide

The Homeland in Aniimo is your personal estate where captured Aniimo contribute their specialized abilities to harvest resources, craft furniture, transport stockpiles, and produce items. Each Aniimo form possesses unique proficiency levels ranging from Level 1 (Novice) to Level 4 (Master / Apex Worker).

📦 Facility Jobs (Estate Infrastructure)

  • Carry (Lv. 1–4): Transports produce, raw ores, and materials directly to Home Storage. Top workers include Inferlupa (Lv. 4), Scorchhowl (Lv. 4), Magmarex (Lv. 4), Waleetle (Lv. 4), and Grizbo (Lv. 4).
  • Artisanship (Lv. 1–4): Crafts furniture, fixtures, and Home appearance items at workbenches. Master artisans include Witchin (Lv. 4), Thornblade (Lv. 4), Blazen (Lv. 4), and Ignitis (Lv. 4).
  • Leisure (Lv. 1–4): Passively generates rare items while engaging in playful activities across recreational zones. Premier producers include Turbo Prismana (Lv. 4), Somniwing (Lv. 4), Stellarys (Lv. 4), and Irisal (Lv. 4).
  • Perfumery (Lv. 1–3): Operates the perfume bench to synthesize scented oils and incense. Specialists are Fragrancier (Lv. 3) and Dewy (Lv. 1).

🌈 Prismana Mutation Balance Mechanics

Prismana forms represent the pinnacle of Homeland efficiency in Aniimo. Based on official in-game and wiki data, Prismana forms strictly observe a canonical stat balance:

  • Primary Element: Escalates to Level 4 (e.g., Wind Lv. 4 on Turbo, Grass Lv. 4 on Somniwing, Dark Lv. 4 on Inferlupa).
  • Facility Job: Reaches Level 4 (e.g., Leisure Lv. 4, Carry Lv. 4, Artisanship Lv. 4).
  • Secondary Mutated Element: Strictly capped at Level 3 (e.g., Dark Lv. 3 on Turbo, Wind Lv. 3 on Somniwing, Fire Lv. 3 on Inferlupa, Ice Lv. 3 on Stellarys).

❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

What are the best Homeland workers in Aniimo?

S-Tier Prismana forms provide the highest efficiency. For hauling, Inferlupa, Scorchhowl, and Magmarex (Carry Lv. 4) are fastest. For crafting, Witchin and Thornblade (Artisanship Lv. 4) lead production. For item generation, Turbo and Somniwing (Leisure Lv. 4) produce top yields.

What Homeland abilities does Turbo Prismana form have?

According to the official Aniimo Wiki, Turbo Prismana Form has Wind Lv. 4, Leisure Lv. 4, and Dark Lv. 3. Its standard base form has Wind Lv. 3 and Leisure Lv. 3.

What Homeland abilities does Somniwing have?

Somniwing (#030) is inherently a Prismana creature featuring Grass Lv. 4, Leisure Lv. 4, and Wind Lv. 3 proficiencies.

Why is Celestis ranked C-Tier in Homeland?

Celestis (#005) is an early unevolved form possessing Dark Lv. 1 and Leisure Lv. 1. Upon evolving into Stellarys (#006), its Prismana form ascends to S-Tier with Dark Lv. 4, Leisure Lv. 4, and Ice Lv. 3.
